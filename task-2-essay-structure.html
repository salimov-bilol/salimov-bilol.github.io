Your introduction is your first impression. It should be concise and powerful, consisting of just two sentences.

  • Sentence 1: Paraphrase the Question. Rephrase the essay topic in your own words to show you understand it.
  • Sentence 2: The Thesis Statement. This is your most important sentence. It must directly answer the question and state your position or the main points you will discuss.

⭐ Expert Tip: A clear thesis is the hallmark of a Band 9 essay. It tells the examiner exactly what to expect and shows you have a well-planned argument right from the start.

This is where you earn the most marks. The golden rule is: one paragraph, one main idea. Don't mix different arguments.

The conclusion should be a short, clean summary of your essay.

  • Sentence 1: Restate Your Thesis. Rephrase your main argument from the introduction using different vocabulary.
  • Sentence 2 (Recommended): Briefly summarize the two main points from your body paragraphs.

⭐ Expert Tip: Never introduce new ideas or arguments in the conclusion. Its sole purpose is to wrap up your essay logically and reinforce the position you've argued.
